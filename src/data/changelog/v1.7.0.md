One new line in Guard's Hard limits: **Today's direction**. Decide before the open, while you are calm, that today you only buy or only sell, and Guard holds you to it for the rest of the trading day.

- **How to set it.** Guard page → Hard limits (switch it on) → Today's direction → **Long only** or **Short only**. Leave the instrument box empty for all instruments (the default), or type one instrument, for example `MNQ` (every expiry of it counts). Guard asks you once to confirm.
- **What Guard does.** A new entry the other way is cancelled. If it fills before the cancel lands (a market order can be faster than NinjaTrader lets Guard cancel it), Guard closes it right away, and only the contracts that order opened. A reversal that goes past your position (long 2, sell 4) goes through for the 2 that close; the 2 it opens the other way are closed right after.
- **What Guard never does.** Closing or reducing a position is never blocked, and Guard never closes a position you already held when you set the direction. Stop and target orders pass.
- **Only tighter during the day.** Once set, it can't be switched off, flipped, or moved to another instrument until 5:00 PM New York time (you can widen one instrument to all instruments). Hard limits can't be switched off while it is set. It clears by itself at 5:00 PM New York, the end of the futures trading day, and is not carried to the next day — set it again each morning. It survives a NinjaTrader restart during the day.
- **It counts you, not an account.** Every account Meridian monitors follows it. A trade copier's copies follow the order they copy, including a reverse copy.
- **When Guard can't tell, it leaves your position alone.** If a fill opens a position the other way and Guard can't match it to an order it saw (for example an order placed while Meridian wasn't running), Guard tells you and does not close it.
- Orders already working when you set the direction are not cancelled; cancel them first. If one fills, Guard closes what it opened.

Also new in Guard: a **Price** condition — stop yourself at a price level you set in advance (NinjaTrader add-on).

- **How to set it.** Add a rule → condition **Price** → type the instrument (for example `MNQ`; Guard watches its current front month as NinjaTrader rolls it), pick **at or below** or **at or above**, and type your level. Then pick what happens: **Acknowledge**, **Pause** or **Disconnect**.
- **It only stops you.** A price rule can't use Notify or Alert. For a plain price alert, NinjaTrader's own Alerts already do that.
- **When it fires.** Once each time the last traded price reaches your level. It fires again only after the price has moved back 2 ticks and reaches the level again. If the price is already past your level when Guard first sees it, it waits until the price comes back and reaches it.
- **It needs live prices in NinjaTrader.** With no live price for the instrument (no data feed, or the feed dropped) the rule does not fire, and the rule on the Guard page says "Not guarding" and why. Losing the feed never makes it fire again by itself, so a Price → Disconnect rule cannot keep disconnecting you on every reconnect. A contract roll is not counted as the price reaching your level.
- Price rules are NinjaTrader add-on only; the standalone app has no live prices, and from version 0.5.0 it shows them as not guarding.

Also in this version: **Monitored accounts lists every connected account.** Settings → Monitored accounts now shows every account connected in NinjaTrader (before, an account Meridian had not picked up — a newly connected demo, for example — never appeared). Tick or untick and save, and it applies right away, no restart. While Guard is locking you or today's direction is set, an account you are trading can't be unticked until that ends.

Also in this version, two fixes for Guard locks that cross 5:00 PM New York (they were prepared as 1.6.1 and ship here):

- **A timed disconnect reconnects at its end, even after 5 PM New York.** If a Guard disconnect is timed (for example "disconnect for 10 minutes") and ends after 5 PM New York, Meridian now reconnects NinjaTrader by itself when it ends. A disconnect that runs to the 5 PM close (rest of day) still leaves NinjaTrader offline until you reconnect.
- **A timed pause that runs past 5 PM New York is shown as a pause** with its own end time, instead of "locked for the day".

Unchanged: your rules, thresholds and the other Hard limits load and behave exactly as before. With Today's direction left unset, Guard behaves exactly as before. If you go back to an older version, the day's direction is dropped and price rules are ignored there (if you change rules in the older version, the price rules are removed); everything else stays.

### How to update

In NinjaTrader: **Tools → Remove NinjaScript Assembly → MeridianPSI → Remove**, then restart NinjaTrader, then **Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On** and pick the new zip. Your sessions, license, baseline and Guard rules stay where they are.
