This release rebuilds how Guard decides when to step in. Guard now follows _you_ — each trade you make, counted once — instead of whatever number an account happens to show at that instant. Every rule is checked on every trade, rules no longer cancel each other out, and copy trading no longer counts one trade several times. Two standing limits are new: **Max trades per day** and **Wait after each trade**. **As always, closing or reducing a position is never blocked — Guard only ever stops you opening new risk.**

Most of the changes below are fixes. A few change what an existing rule does, so please read them — especially if you trade more than one account.

### New: two standing limits in Hard limits

- **Max trades per day.** Reach your number and new trades are blocked until 5:00 PM New York time. Exits and stops always go through. A trade copied to several accounts counts once. Your HUD shows "Trade 2/3" only when this limit is on; otherwise it shows your pace (for example "4 trades · pace 3"), not a limit.
- **Wait after each trade.** After a trade closes — win or lose — new entries are blocked for the minutes you choose. Want a break only after losses? Use a "Consecutive losses ≥ 1 → pause" rule; it now fires on every loss.
- **Strict lock now also protects your Hard limits.** While Strict lock is on, your Hard limits can only be made stricter, not looser.

### What changes in how Guard behaves

- **Every trade is checked, even while a pause is running.** Before, while one rule had you paused, Guard didn't check your other rules. Now it does: if a rule that locks you for the rest of the day is hit during a short pause, the day lock starts right away.
- **When several rules fire at once, all of them count.** The lock lasts as long as the longest one and uses the strictest mode (disconnect beats cancel). Before, only the first rule in your list took effect.
- **An "Acknowledge" rule that fires during a pause now waits for the pause to end.** The prompt appears the moment the pause ends, and new entries stay blocked until you acknowledge. If the pause runs to the end of the day, the prompt is dropped.
- **"Single trade loss over $X" fires on every trade that crosses it.** Before, two big losses in a row could trigger it only once.
- **"Consecutive losses" resets on a win.** Before, a win only reduced the count, so the rule could fire late. A break-even trade neither counts as a loss nor resets the streak. Each rule uses its own "Only count losses above $".
- **Copy trading: one order copied to several accounts is one trade.** Your loss streak, trade count, single-trade loss and PSI all count it once. Its P&L is the P&L on your lead account, and so is your floating loss.
- **Daily loss with several accounts now counts you, not each account.** Since 1.5.7, with two or more accounts Guard stopped only the account that hit the limit, without a popup, and ignored the action you chose. Now your daily loss is the total of your trades today — each copied trade counted once, at your lead account's P&L — and Guard does exactly what your rule says, on all your accounts.
- **Practice accounts don't count against your real trading.** If you're connected to a real or prop account, trades on NinjaTrader's built-in simulator accounts (Sim101 and the like) don't count toward your trade count, daily loss, streak or PSI. If you only trade on Sim, they count as before. Guard locks still block new entries on Sim too.
- **Copies no longer drag PSI down.** One order copied to several accounts is now one entry for PSI, so the copies no longer count as extra trades the moment you enter. PSI also times each entry from that trade's own fill.
- **PSI measures hold time on NinjaTrader's clock.** If your computer and NinjaTrader were set to different time zones, PSI could count a position you had just opened as held for hours (or a long hold as just opened). Hold time is now measured on NinjaTrader's own clock.
- **Reversing while you're locked only closes your position.** A reverse order still goes through so you can get out, but the new position it opens is closed right after it fills.
- **"Today" means the futures trading day.** Daily loss, trade count and loss streak reset at 5:00 PM New York time. The Session history page and PSI still start a new day at your local midnight.
- **Force reset ends only the lock you're in.** Before, it also disarmed rules that hadn't fired yet.
- **"Trading for more than N minutes" counts from your first trade of the trading day**, on one clock, and a reconnect doesn't reset it. Before, it counted from the first fill of the session and could be off by hours when your computer and NinjaTrader were set to different time zones.
- **Restarting NinjaTrader doesn't reset today.** Your trade count, daily P&L, streak and any running lock survive a restart, and a lock that already ended won't fire again because you restarted.
- **Guard shows the day and time a lock ends.** The HUD banner, the Guard page, the Strict lock card and Guard's lock messages give the end of a lock as a day, date and time on your computer's clock — for example "locked for the day · resumes Mon 19 Oct 22:00", or for a timed pause "new entries blocked until Mon 19 Oct 15:47 · 12:34 left". Before, they showed only a time such as "17:00", and a timed pause showed only a countdown. A message still on screen the next morning shows exactly when the lock ended.
- **Trades made while Meridian was closed now count.** When NinjaTrader re-sends today's fills on startup or after a reconnect, Guard adds any trades it hadn't seen — including trades you made elsewhere while Meridian was closed. If one of them breaks a rule, the lock starts right away for the time it has left (counted from that trade); a lock that would already have ended is skipped.
- **If a position closes without Guard seeing the closing fill,** that trade is no longer counted as a $0 trade; it's left out of single-trade loss and the loss streak.
- **The starter rules from first-time setup no longer have a hidden delay.**
- **Max contracts now counts your current position.** Before, an add-on order was only blocked if that one order was over the limit.

Unchanged: your rules, thresholds and actions load exactly as you saved them. Exits are never blocked. Turning Guard off still leaves your Hard limits on.

### How to update

In NinjaTrader: **Tools → Remove NinjaScript Assembly → MeridianPSI → Remove**, then restart NinjaTrader, then **Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On** and pick the new zip. Your sessions, license, baseline and Guard rules stay where they are.
