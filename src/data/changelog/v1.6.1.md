Two fixes for Guard locks that cross 5:00 PM New York time, the end of the futures trading day.

- **A timed disconnect reconnects at its end, even after 5 PM New York.** If a Guard disconnect is timed (for example "disconnect for 10 minutes") and ends after 5 PM New York, Meridian now reconnects NinjaTrader by itself when it ends, as it does at any other time of day. A disconnect that runs to the 5 PM close (rest of day) still leaves NinjaTrader offline until you reconnect.
- **A timed pause that runs past 5 PM New York is shown as a pause.** The HUD banner, the Guard page and the "Still locked" message now show it as the pause it is, with its own end time, instead of "locked for the day". Only a lock that ends at the 5 PM close says "locked for the day".

Unchanged: your rules, thresholds and Hard limits; how long every lock lasts. Closing or reducing a position is never blocked.

### How to update

In NinjaTrader: **Tools → Remove NinjaScript Assembly → MeridianPSI → Remove**, then restart NinjaTrader, then **Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On** and pick the new zip. Your sessions, license, baseline and Guard rules stay where they are.
