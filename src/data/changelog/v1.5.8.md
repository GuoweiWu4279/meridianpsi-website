Meridian Core — live PSI, the HUD, Session Report, History, Journal and the full Intel workspace — is now free. Get a free license key (email only, no card), paste it into the License page, and everything in Core unlocks. Guard, the part that enforces your rules, is $29.99 a month. This release updates the app to match.

### What changed in the app

- **The unlock screen** you see before activating now says what is true: Core is free, you get a free license key in a minute, and Guard is free for your first 7 days on a new free license, then $29.99 a month. The button opens the free-license page.
- **The License page** shows the free license first, then what Guard adds. The old "upgrade for full access" card is gone — Intel, History and the Journal were never Guard features, and now none of Core costs anything.
- **License activation now checks your key through meridianpsi.com** instead of contacting the store directly. The result is the same; there are fewer moving parts between your key and the check. If your firewall only lets Meridian reach specific hosts, allow `www.meridianpsi.com`.

Nothing about PSI, your baselines, your Guard rules or your history changes. **As always, closing or reducing a position is never blocked — Guard only ever stops you opening new risk.**

### How to update

In NinjaTrader: **Tools → Remove NinjaScript Assembly → MeridianPSI → Remove**, then restart NinjaTrader, then **Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On** and pick the new zip. Your sessions, license, baseline and Guard rules stay where they are.
