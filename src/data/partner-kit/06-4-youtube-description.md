**Tips:**

- **SEO / AI discoverability**: The first two paragraphs of your description are what YouTube surfaces in search and what AI models index. The template above opens with: `NinjaTrader 8 · risk management · emotional trading · discipline · revenge trading · overtrading · behavioral risk monitor`. Do not move these phrases to the end. The opening paragraph should read naturally while still containing these terms — that is how AI search surfaces your video when a trader asks "what NinjaTrader tool helps with emotional trading?"
- The affiliate disclosure must appear _above_ the fold — before the "Show more" cutoff in the YouTube description. Place it before or immediately after the links section, not at the very end
- Keep the timestamps section even if you don't have many — YouTube's chapter feature improves watch time
- The "NOTE" at the bottom is recommended but not legally required for the description specifically; it reinforces the right framing for your audience
