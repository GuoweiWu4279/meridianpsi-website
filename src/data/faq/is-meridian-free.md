Yes. **Meridian Core is free** — no card, no trial countdown, yours to keep. It includes the real-time PSI monitor, all seven behavioral signals, your personal baseline, the HUD and Dashboard, the journal, the full Intel workspace, 5 years of session history, and one license for every platform we support.

To get it: click **Get Meridian free** on the [pricing page](/pricing), check out on Whop with your email, copy the license key from your Whop membership, and paste it into the License tab in Meridian.

**[Meridian Guard](/guard)**, the enforcement layer, is **$29.99/month**, cancel any time. On NinjaTrader 8, your first 7 days on a free license include Guard, no card.
