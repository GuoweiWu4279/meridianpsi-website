---
question: 'How do I cancel my subscription?'
category: billing
order: 2
---

You can cancel your Guard subscription any time — no cancellation fees and no questions asked. Guard stays on until the end of the period you have paid for, and your free Core license keeps working.

To cancel: visit [whop.com/@me/settings/memberships](https://whop.com/@me/settings/memberships), log in with the email you used at checkout, and you will see your memberships. From there you can cancel, update your payment method, or access your License Key.

If you run into any trouble, email us at [contactmeridianpsi@gmail.com](mailto:contactmeridianpsi@gmail.com) and we will handle it manually within 24 hours.
